# WF-006 — Measure and maintain

Trigger: verified publication, scheduled review, incident, source change, system change, usage signal, or performance evidence.  
Outcome: evidence-based lifecycle decision and next review cycle.  
Accountable role: content or program owner.

| Step | Actor | Action | Output/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 6.1 | Evaluation owner | Collect only approved measures and state what each measure can and cannot demonstrate. | evaluation evidence |
| 6.2 | Evaluation owner | Compare evidence with the original performance outcome and target. | findings |
| 6.3 | Content owner | Verify source currency, interface/process changes, accessibility, support issues, discoverability, and audience fit. | maintenance review |
| 6.4 | Reviewer | Classify findings, material changes, risks, and required regression scope. | finding/change records |
| 6.5 | Lifecycle authority | Decide continue, continue with conditions, revise, constrain, pause, replace, or retire. | G11 decision |
| 6.6 | Content owner | Assign actions, dates, owners, evidence requirements, and next review trigger. | lifecycle record |
| 6.7 | Repository custodian | Preserve the current baseline and archive superseded records according to policy. | controlled history |

Required gate: `G11`.

Attendance, completion, activity, satisfaction, knowledge, task competence, task performance, transfer, and organizational results are distinct evidence levels. Do not present lower-level measures as proof of higher-level effects.
