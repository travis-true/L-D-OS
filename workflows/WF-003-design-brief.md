# WF-003 — Design and brief

Trigger: approved solution recommendation.  
Outcome: approved intervention blueprint and build-ready specification.  
Accountable role: learning designer.

| Step | Actor | Action | Output/evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3.1 | Designer | Convert the approved recommendation into observable performance outcomes. | objectives with conditions and success criteria |
| 3.2 | Designer | Identify moments of need, recall/reference need, frequency/criticality, skill type, shelf life, and reach. | format-routing evidence |
| 3.3 | Designer | Select the smallest effective intervention and explain tradeoffs. | format/modality decision |
| 3.4 | Designer | Define content scope, exclusions, sequence, practice, assessment, accessibility, evaluation, support, and maintenance. | design record |
| 3.5 | Designer | Select relevant standards from `standards/registry.json`; record non-applicable items and rationale. | standards map |
| 3.6 | Reviewer | Verify alignment among problem, outcomes, intervention, practice, assessment, and planned evaluation. | design-review evidence |
| 3.7 | Design authorizer | Approve, approve with conditions, or return the design. | G04 decision |
| 3.8 | Production lead | Create asset specifications, acceptance criteria, roles, dependencies, and version plan. | asset records and G05 decision |

Required gates: `G04`, `G05`. Fast-track may combine the two decisions but must record both outcomes.

Prototype early when stakeholder expectations, learner behavior, or interface details are uncertain.
