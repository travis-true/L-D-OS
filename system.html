Governance

Core principles

Diagnose first

Requester preferences are evidence, not the final solution decision. Determine whether training is appropriate before building it.

Ground every decision

Use approved sources and distinguish facts, assumptions, inferences, conflicts, gaps, and decisions required.

Keep authority human

Automation may draft, classify, compare, and recommend. It may not approve gates, waive controls, accept risk, or authorize release.

Separate QA and release

A quality disposition describes evidence. A named human authority decides whether the exact candidate may be released.

Verify publication

Publishing is complete only when the authorized asset is retrieved through the intended audience path.

Maintain ownership

Every operational asset has an owner, review trigger, measurement plan, and lifecycle disposition.

Seven-stage surface

Workflows

1

Intake

Authorize scope, capture the need, and select the operating lane.

2

Brief

Govern sources, diagnose causes, recommend the smallest effective intervention, and approve design.

3

Build

Authorize specifications, create versioned candidates, and preserve traceability.

4

Review

Freeze the candidate, perform independent QA, record findings, and retest repairs.

5

Publish

Obtain human release authorization and verify retrieval through the intended channel.

6

Measure

Collect approved performance and business evidence without treating completion as transfer.

7

Maintain

Review sources and outcomes, control changes, and continue, revise, replace, or retire.
